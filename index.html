Chained Echoes — Class Emblem Browser

A reference for Chained Echoes. All 12 Class Emblems' stat trends, granted skills, and statue locations in one place — built for the moment you're standing at a statue with one Sacred Water and no easy way to compare options. This page does not rank emblems or produce any kind of verdict pick — it is a browser, not a tier list. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere; everything renders from the data in this page in your browser.

Pick a character

Sources & data honesty

Numeric stat data: no source reached — commonsensegamer.com's emblem guide, arrpeegeez.com's walkthrough, gameranx.com's two guides, gameplay.tips's guide, a Steam Community thread, or a per-character build-recommendation article series — gives an exact numeric value for any Class Emblem's stat bonus.
